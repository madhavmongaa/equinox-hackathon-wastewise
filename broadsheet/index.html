Letters

Dear Lulu

The questions everyone has at the bin, answered the way Lulu answers them: the bin, then what to do before it goes in.

“The pizza box has cheese stuck to it.”

Two bins. Tear off the clean lid for the blue bin. The greasy base goes in the green one with food waste.

“A dead AA battery from the TV remote.”

Red bin, hazardous. Tape both ends and keep it well away from the dry bin.

“My old phone charger stopped working.”

E-waste drop box. Never the dry bin. A kabadiwala may even pay you for the copper.

“Used tea bags, every morning.”

Green bin. Pull out the staple first if yours has one.

“Diapers and sanitary pads.”

Grey bin, reject. Wrap them in paper and mark the packet so whoever collects it knows.

“I honestly don’t know what this thing is.”

Then I’ll ask you one question first. I won’t guess a bin I’m not sure of.

Ask by typing or by voice, in English or Hindi. Every answer is AI-generated, so check an unusual one against your local rules.
